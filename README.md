# Habit Tracker

My Personal, modern, fast, and responsive web application for daily habit tracking and visualizing what you can achieve in one year through consistent progress.

## Key Features

* **Habit & Category Management:** Organize habits into custom categories (e.g., Morning, During the day, Evening, Other) with customizable weekly targets.
* **Interactive Tracking:** Quick and simple daily check-ins.
* **Streak Freeze:** Protects your earned streaks on busy days.
* **Detailed Statistics:** Weekly and monthly charts along with an activity heatmap.
* **Achievements & Milestones:** Rewards for consistent progress.
* **Smooth Swipe Actions:** Easily swipe left on mobile or desktop cards for quick editing or deletion.
* **Data Management:** Export your complete tracking history in CSV format.
* **PWA Support:** Installable directly to your home screen for a native app experience.
